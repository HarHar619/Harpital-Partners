About

What we do

Harcapital is an investment firm providing venture and private equity capital to technology companies and enduring businesses. We partner with founders and operators to allocate capital with discipline, clarity, and a long-term ownership mindset.

Our approach favors concentrated relationships and rigorous underwriting. We bring institutional process, patient capital, and aligned partnership to each commitment, and remain engaged where judgment beyond the check is useful.

Philosophy

How we invest

Investment quality begins with the business itself. We look for companies with a durable edge in product, brand, distribution, or cost structure; leadership that allocates capital carefully; and unit economics that hold without heroic assumptions. Growth is welcome when it is earned. Scale without substance is not.

We remain flexible on stage. Early venture, growth equity, and private equity situations can all fit when the opportunity is strong and the partnership is aligned. Price matters. A strong business bought carelessly is still a weak investment. We prefer to wait rather than force capital into something that fails our bar.

Qualitative judgment carries as much weight as the model. We spend time with management, customers, and the competitive landscape. We revisit the thesis as facts change. If the thesis still holds and the price is better, we add. If it does not, we exit. Holding cash when nothing meets the standard is an acceptable outcome.

What we look for

High-conviction investments tend to share several traits:

  • A durable competitive position with clear reasons it should persist
  • Attractive markets with room to grow without relying on fashion
  • Leadership that communicates plainly and compounds shareholder value
  • A path to sustainable margins and cash generation, not growth at any cost
  • Valuation that leaves room for error relative to expected outcomes
  • Alignment between founders, operators, and capital over a long horizon

Competitive businesses of this kind are uncommon. Concentration is a feature: it lets us study each investment carefully and stay close to the people running it.

Focus

Where we spend our time

Our primary interest is technology-enabled businesses and growth companies with enduring economics. That includes software and digital infrastructure, applied automation where the use case is commercial rather than speculative, and traditional businesses being reshaped by better tools, distribution, or operating discipline.

We are also open to private equity situations in companies with solid fundamentals that need patient ownership, operational support, or a clearer capital structure. Sector labels matter less than whether the business can create lasting value and whether we can be a constructive partner.

Themes we return to often:

  • B2B software and platforms with sticky workflows and expanding use
  • Infrastructure and tools that make other companies more productive
  • Consumer or marketplace businesses with real brand and retention
  • Industrial and services companies where technology lifts margins and reach
  • Growth equity and selective PE where ownership and incentives are clear
